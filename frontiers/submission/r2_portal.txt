Q4 Newer regulations. See 6, L683-690; 2, L131-144. Regulatory change is now identified as the most informative extension of the design, and we name the mechanism rather than the regulation alone: Article 73 of the EU AI Act requires providers of high-risk AI systems to report serious incidents to national market-surveillance authorities, creating a mandatory incident record that can be compared with securities disclosure exactly as this study compares a media-derived one. Re-applying the design under that regime, or under future U.S. guidance such as an Item 1.05-style current-reporting requirement, would test whether mandatory reporting narrows the gap between externally recorded events and incidents identifiable in filings. The present estimates therefore serve as a pre-regime baseline. L131-144 introduce the EU AI Act earlier, where it contrasts with the U.S. cybersecurity regime.
